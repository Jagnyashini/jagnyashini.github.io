<h2 class="research-title">Low Power IoT-Communication</h2>
<p class="research-desc"> We design lightweight communication protocols for reliable, real-time data sharing among resource-constrained devices in low-power IoT networks. Our research develops scalable synchronous-transmission-based solutions that adapt to underlying topological changes and support fault-tolerant operation. Through in-parallel data-sharing strategies and local coordination, these protocols reduce communication latency and energy consumption for large scale dynamic deployments.</p>

<h2 class="research-title">Distributed UAV Swarm Network</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Designing distributed coordination frameworks for autonomous UAV swarms, enabling real-time cluster formation, rapid topological organisation and scalable multi-drone data collection. By combining efficient inter-drone communication with IoT-assisted coordination, our research supports adaptive network formation, reliable information exchange and collaborative aerial operations across dynamic, large-scale and resource-constrained environments with minimal overhead.</p>

<h2 class="research-title">Distributed Machine Learning and Edge Intelligence</h2>
<p class="research-desc">Design of distributed framework for execution of complex machine-learning (ML) on resource-constrained IoT edge networks. Complex ML models are partitioned and collaboratively executed across ultra-low-power devices. Efficient local communication enables scalable training, low-latency inference and reduced per-device memory usage. Redundant execution paths deliver resilient and fault-tolerant intelligence at the network edge. </p>

<h2 class="research-title">Smart IoT Applications</h2>
<p class="research-desc">We develop smart IoT applications that combine lightweight sensing, distributed coordination and real-time decision-making for transportation and energy systems. <h2 class="research-title">Digital Twins for IoT Network</h2>
<p class="research-desc">We develop efficient Digital Twin frameworks that synchronise physical IoT entities with their virtual replicas through rapid, energy-efficient data collection. By combining concurrent transmission, distributed device coordination and UAV-assisted sensing, our research reduces aerial movement and communication delay, adapts to topology changes and improves physical-to-digital mapping across large-scale dynamic networks reliably.</p>
